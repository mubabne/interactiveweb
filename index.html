(02)Our story

We're a neighbourhood coffee house obsessed with the small stuff — the grind, the bloom, the eighteen seconds before the shot turns. Every cup is pulled by hand, served without hurry, and refilled before you ask.

Founded in a converted print shop in 2014, Ember roasts twice a week on a 12-kilo drum. Nothing sits on the shelf longer than ten days.
